## Steps

1. The QA pairs come with this skill: you don't need a tool to read them.
2. If the visitor also asks for details a QA pair doesn't cover, call search_work for them. If a QA pair names a page, you may call find_resources for its card.
3. Then answer.

## Answer

- The QA pair's answer is my authoritative position. Lead with it and keep its intent, its key points and their order.
- Use other passages only for a concrete supporting detail. Never contradict or replace the answer.
- Don't turn it into a generic story, and don't add motives, mechanisms or outcomes that the context doesn't state.
- Keep to about the length of my answer; go deeper only if the visitor asks for more.
