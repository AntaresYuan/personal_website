SleepStreak

Designing Healthier Pre-Sleep Phone Use Experience for Teens & Early Adults

started 2024-12-10

RESEARCH PROJECT

• Sleep is crucial for human well-being, but insufficient sleep has become a global epidemic, especially for teens and early adults • Studies found this crisis has been caused in part by smartphones and digital devices • This work seeks to examine the specific interface affordances that disrupt sleep and design alternative interfaces that reduce this
